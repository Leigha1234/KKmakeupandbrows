Bridal questions

Good to
know.

Is a bridal preview included?

The £395 bridal package includes a 60–90 minute bridal preview before the wedding day.

How many people are included?

The package covers the bride plus three additional people. Additional makeup is listed at £60 per person, up to six people in one booking.

Does Kara travel?

Travel up to one hour is included in the bridal package. The supplied guide lists £40 per additional hour beyond that.

How do I secure my date?

The bridal guide lists a £100 non-refundable booking deposit, deducted from the final balance. Contact Kara to confirm availability and current terms.
